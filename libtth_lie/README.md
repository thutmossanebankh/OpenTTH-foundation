# libtth-lie : Algebra of Multi-centric Transformations (C++)

`libtth-lie` is the module dedicated to validating the Lie Algebra underlying the generalized TTH.

## ⚙️ Features

* Numerical proof of formal non-commutativity: $T_1 \circ T_2 \neq T_2 \circ T_1$.
* Infinitesimal calculation of the Lie bracket (commutator): $[T_1, T_2] = T_1 \circ T_2 - T_2 \circ T_1$.
* Extraction and visualization (via Python) of the generating vector field induced by multi-centrism.
