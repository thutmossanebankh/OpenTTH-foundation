 tth-fouriergeom : Discrete Harmonic Analysis (Python)

This module demonstrates the profound isomorphism between polygonal multi-centric geometry and signal theory.

## ⚙️ Features

* Numerical proof of the **Quadratic Invariance Theorem** for regular polygons (strict conservation of the barycenter via cyclic cancellation).
* Modeling of the geometric DFT (Discrete Fourier Transform): decomposition of the vertices on the basis of the $n$-th roots of unity.
* Spatial filtering algorithm: smoothing of noisy contours by cancellation of high-frequency deformation modes.
